Data: GeoDevelopment Observatory of Bolivia (geo4bolivia), from Andersen, L. E. & Argandoña, F. (2026), Atlas de los ODS en Bolivia 2026, SDSN Bolivia. SDG 13 data · SDG 15 data.

Method: PCA on ranks of the nine indicators, standardised, for the 190 municipalities with forest data (2024 levels, and 2012→2024 changes). Correlations: Pearson for levels, Spearman for changes. Typology: sign of the level PC1 and change PC1 scores. Values in years "2012" and "2024" come from slightly different years per indicator. Analysis by Kokila Hemachadra, GSID, Nagoya University.
